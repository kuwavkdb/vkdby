# See https://www.robotstxt.org/robotstxt.html for documentation on how to use the robots.txt file

# Searching (q) and filtering (tag_ids etc.) on the people / units lists, and site search,
# have practically unlimited combinations and cause heavy queries when crawled (issue #1439, #1855).
# Individual people / units pages are reachable from the sitemap.
User-agent: *
Disallow: /people?*q=
Disallow: /people?*tag_ids
Disallow: /people?*part=
Disallow: /people?*blood=
Disallow: /people?*hometown=
Disallow: /people?*status=
Disallow: /units?*q=
Disallow: /units?*tag_ids
Disallow: /search

Sitemap: https://vkdb.jp/sitemap.xml
